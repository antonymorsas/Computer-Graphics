The trick

Everything hangs from a base group (an Object3D). Dragging or scaling only touches that group, and the objects, rings and moons inside inherit its spin and size.

The moons

Each moon lives in its own group, placed where the last object is and hung from the base group. That group spins on its own, so the moon circles its object while the whole system spins.

Made with

Three.js: nested groups, random shapes (dodecahedron, icosahedron, octahedron and sphere) with Phong material, rings made with RingGeometry, and a directional light plus an ambient one.
