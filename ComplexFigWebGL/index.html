Computer Graphics · 2020

Shapes

Three solids built vertex by vertex in WebGL, with no 3D library: a pyramid with a pentagonal base, a dodecahedron and an octahedron, each spinning on its own axis.

Speed

Space pauses too

The shapes

  • Pentagonal a pyramid with a 5-sided base and 5 triangular faces rising to the tip.
  • Dodecahedron 12 pentagonal faces; its 20 vertices come from the golden ratio.
  • Octahedron 8 triangular faces; besides spinning, it bobs up and down.

By hand

Each shape is a list of vertices, one color per face and a list of indices saying which vertices make each triangle. Pentagons are split into three triangles.

Made with

Plain WebGL with hand-written shaders and gl-matrix for the matrices: a 45° perspective projection and one rotation per shape every frame. At 1x they turn once every 5 seconds.
